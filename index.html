Our Story

Invercargill's Go-To Butcher Since Day One

Mighty Meats has been serving Invercargill and the wider Southland region with the best quality lamb and beef we can get our hands on. We know this region's meat like the back of our hand, because we've been right here through all of it.

Whether you're feeding the family a Sunday roast, stocking the restaurant freezer, or loading up for a big BBQ, we cut fresh to order and don't mess around with quality. You get what's on the label, every time.

Talk to Corey and the team, and you'll know straight away you're dealing with people who take their meat seriously.
