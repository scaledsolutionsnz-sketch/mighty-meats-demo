Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Who We Are

Mighty Meats is a butcher and meat supplier based in Invercargill, New Zealand. This privacy policy explains how we handle information collected through our website.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a contact or feedback form on this website, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details (email, phone)
  • Your enquiry or message content
  • Product or service preferences you select

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry, process your order request, or address your feedback. We do not use your information for marketing without your consent.

4. Third-Party Services

This website uses StaticForms to process form submissions. Form data is transmitted securely to their service for delivery to us. Please refer to StaticForms' privacy policy for details on how they handle data.

5. Data Retention

We retain contact information only as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry or fulfil your order. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.

6. Your Rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to access and correct personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at southlamb@out.com.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at southlamb@out.com or call 03 216 1332.
